---

## 2. Customer-facing rules

1. Every primary screen shows **OpsEdge360** branding  
2. About / login / reports / exports include **Powered by AsoftechInsightz**  
3. **No third-party product branding** in customer UI (no SkyWalking, Wazuh, GLPI, NetBox, n8n, Ansible marks or default themes)  
4. Connector admin may use **neutral** names (“Observability engine”, “Security engine”) — optional technical ID in advanced panel for admins only  
5. Do not fork or rebrand upstream projects; do not claim their trademarks  

---

## 3. Voice & tone

- Executive-clear, precise, calm under incident pressure  
- Prefer “degraded / critical / healthy” over slang  
- AI content labeled advisory where appropriate  

---

## 4. Visual identity (product)

- Own design tokens and components  
- Dark/light enterprise themes  
- Luxury-minimal executive surfaces; dense operator modes where needed  
- See [OPSEDGE360_UX_DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md](./OPSEDGE360_UX_DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md)  

---

## 5. Solution packs

Packs may use names like **Banking360** as **OpsEdge360 solution packs** — still under OpsEdge360 shell, not separate products in the UX chrome.

---

## 6. Legal / docs

- Preserve upstream licenses in distribution of engines (engine plane)  
- Customer docs describe OpsEdge360 capabilities, not “how to use Wazuh UI”  
- Trademark diligence for competitive comparisons in marketing (separate from this architecture pack)
