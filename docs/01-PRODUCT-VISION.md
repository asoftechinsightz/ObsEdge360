## 1. Executive Summary

OpsEdge360 is the world's first **AI-Native Autonomous Enterprise Intelligence Platform** that unifies IT, OT, Network, Cloud, Security, Business Transactions, Digital Twin, CMDB, Compliance, Sustainability, and Quantum Readiness into a single intelligent system.

The platform eliminates manual discovery and static documentation by continuously observing, learning, and acting across the entire enterprise stack — from customer-facing transactions to factory floor PLCs.

## 2. Problem Statement

Modern enterprises operate fragmented observability, CMDB, security, and compliance tools that:

- Require manual asset registration and reconciliation
- Cannot correlate IT incidents with business transaction impact
- Lack visibility into OT/IoT alongside cloud and on-prem
- Produce alert fatigue without actionable intelligence
- Fail to map compliance evidence to live infrastructure state
- Cannot answer "what is the business impact?" in real time

## 3. Vision Statement

> **One platform that automatically knows what exists, how it connects, who owns it, what it costs, whether it is compliant, and what to do when something breaks — before humans need to ask.**

## 7. Platform Questions Answered

The platform automatically answers:

- What assets exist? Who owns them? Where are they?
- How are they connected? Which services depend on them?
- Which business transactions use them?
- What is their health, compliance status, and risk score?
- What threats, fraud indicators, and anomalies exist?
- What is the business and environmental impact of failure?
- What actions should be taken? Which can be safely automated?

## 10. Success Criteria

The platform shall:

- Automatically discover the enterprise without manual CMDB entry
- Maintain a live, accurate CMDB and digital twin
- Map end-to-end business transactions with correlated telemetry
- Detect anomalies, fraud, and compliance violations proactively
- Predict incidents and quantify business impact
- Recommend and automate remediation where policy permits
- Serve CIO, CISO, COO, CFO, and operations from one platform
