# Leadership Focus Recommendation

With Engineering Freeze v1.0 and CVP in place, recommended personal / leadership split:

| Focus | Share |
|-------|------:|
| Product engineering oversight (bugs, security, performance, customer-requested) | **~20%** |
| Enterprise sales, partnerships, customer discovery, pilot execution, fundraising | **~80%** |

Gartner-level recognition follows **successful enterprise adoption**, not only technical sophistication.
