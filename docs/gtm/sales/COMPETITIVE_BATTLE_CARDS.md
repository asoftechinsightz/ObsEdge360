# Competitive Battle Cards

> Keep factual. Update after each competitive loss/win. Do not invent fake benchmarks.

## vs Fragmented DIY (Prometheus + Grafana + spreadsheets + ITSM)

| Them | Us |
|------|-----|
| Flexible but high glue cost | Productized ops intelligence + pilot packaging |
| Weak executive narrative | Dashboard + reports + Copilot briefing |
| Security bolted on later | MFA, SSO, session revoke, SBOM, assessments |

**Landmine:** “We already have Grafana.” → “Great—OpsEdge360 consumes signals and adds CMDB/ITSM/compliance/exec risk; we document self-observability scrape targets too.”

## vs Single-domain APM vendors

| Them | Us |
|------|-----|
| Deep code-level APM | Cross-domain ops + config + compliance packs |
| Cloud SaaS-first | On-prem / HA / air-gap patterns |

**Landmine:** “We need code traces.” → Partner or coexist; don’t claim full APM replacement unless scoped.

## vs Mega ITSM suites

| Them | Us |
|------|-----|
| Process system of record | Operational intelligence feeding ITSM language |
| Heavy implementations | Faster pilot path with clear exit criteria |

**Landmine:** “Replace ServiceNow.” → No—integrate / complement; win on observability + risk + RCA speed.

## vs Horizontal “AIOps” platforms

| Them | Us |
|------|-----|
| Broad AI claims | Concrete Copilot assist + honest limitations |
| Long professional services | Commercial + EPP kits for self-serve evaluation |

**Always disclose:** known limitations (Helm subset, simulated browser synthetics, modeled large-scale soak).
