# Pricing Guide (Internal Framework)

> **Internal use.** Finalize commercial numbers with finance before customer quote. This framework standardizes packaging language.

## Packaging model (recommended)

| Edition | Intent | Typical inclusion |
|---------|--------|-------------------|
| Pilot | Time-boxed EPP | Single env, success criteria, CS office hours |
| Standard | Production departmental / mid-size | Compose HA, SSO, MFA, core modules |
| Enterprise | Multi-env, regulated, HA | HA, air-gap option, premium support, Banking360 pack |
| Air-gap add-on | Offline delivery | Air-gap package + verify + premium onboarding |

## Metric options (pick one primary)

1. **Nodes / CIs under management**  
2. **Platform subscription (org) + optional packs** (Banking360)  
3. **Named environments** (prod / non-prod)  

## Discounting guardrails

- Pilot fees may be credited to Year-1 subscription on conversion.  
- No perpetual “free unlimited POC” without exit date.  
- Partner margin: see Partner Program Guide.  

## Quote checklist

- [ ] Edition + term (12/36 months)  
- [ ] Environments count  
- [ ] Support tier  
- [ ] Packs / add-ons  
- [ ] Professional services days (optional)  
- [ ] Known limitations acknowledged
