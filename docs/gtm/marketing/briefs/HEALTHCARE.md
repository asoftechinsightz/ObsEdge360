# Industry Solution Brief — Healthcare

**OpsEdge360** supports healthcare IT operations where uptime and auditability matter: unified visibility across clinical/adjacent infrastructure domains, synthetics on patient-critical portals, and ITSM-aligned incident response.

## Use cases

- Availability of patient-facing digital channels  
- Change/incident correlation for clinical systems adjacency  
- Executive briefing during incidents  

## Pilot suggestion

Non-PHI demo data · strict tenancy · SSO · scoped synthetics.

Handle PHI/PII per customer policy; platform hosts where customer deploys.
