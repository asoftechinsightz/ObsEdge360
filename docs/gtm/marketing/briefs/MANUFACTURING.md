# Industry Solution Brief — Manufacturing

**OpsEdge360** helps manufacturing IT/OT-adjacent teams see business impact of infrastructure and application health—plant systems interfaces, supplier portals, and MES-adjacent services—without pretending to be a full OT historian.

## Use cases

- Digital channel / plant portal synthetics  
- Incident blast-radius framing for production IT  
- Executive ops reviews  

## Pilot suggestion

Start with IT-managed systems; OT integration only with clear ownership and safety review.
