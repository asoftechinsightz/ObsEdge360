# Reseller Guide

## Ideal reseller

Sells enterprise infra / observability / ITSM adjacency; can reach CIO/SRE buyers.

## Deal flow

1. Register opportunity (account, ICP fit).  
2. Joint discovery using sales questionnaire.  
3. AsoftechInsightz-led or partner-led demo (script).  
4. Pilot via EPP pack.  
5. Procurement pack to InfoSec.  
6. Close with approved price book.  

## Rules

- No gray-market builds from random forks—use attested commercial packages.  
- Disclose limitations.  
- Implementation may be subcontracted to certified implementation partners.

## Enablement

Demo script, battle cards, pricing guardrails, partner portal content checklist.
