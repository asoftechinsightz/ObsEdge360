# Content Guide — Marketing v2.1

## Brand voice

- Confident, precise, executive — not startup hype.
- Outcomes before features.
- Never claim unavailable mobile apps or Planned integrations as GA.

## Naming

| Use | Avoid |
|-----|-------|
| AsoftechInsightz | Abbreviated slang brands |
| Asoftech Business Suite | “our tool” |
| OpsEdge360 / LeadEdge360 / RetailEdge360 | Observability360 |
| OpenTelemetry / REST | Vendor engine marketing |

## Page patterns

1. **PageHero** — eyebrow + H1 + short support  
2. Capability grids / interactive previews  
3. Outcomes / FAQs  
4. **CtaBand** — demo / sales  

## CTAs (preferred)

- Book a Demo  
- Request Pricing  
- Contact Sales  
- Schedule Consultation  
- Download Brochure (resources)

## Industry pages

Always include: challenges · outcomes · recommended products · AI · case-style story · CTA.

## Legal / claims

- RetailEdge360: **In Development**  
- Mobile apps: **Coming Soon**  
- Integrations: mark **Planned** vs **Available**
