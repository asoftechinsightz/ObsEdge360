# Strategic Pause — Market Proof Over Milestones

**Effective:** 2026-07-12  
**Product:** OpsEdge360 v1.0 Enterprise Platform  
**Status:** Feature-complete enough to enter the market under Engineering Freeze  

## Final verdict

OpsEdge360 v1.0 is **feature-complete enough to enter the market**.

It does **not** claim feature parity with every Gartner Leader — that bar requires years of customer validation, ecosystem maturity, and operational scale.

The limiting factor is **no longer building the product**.  
The limiting factor is **proving the product in real enterprise environments**.

From this point onward, the roadmap is driven by **customer evidence**, not internal feature ideas.

That is the transition from building software to building a successful enterprise software business.

## 90–180 day focus (primary)

| Priority | Activity |
|----------|----------|
| 1 | Enterprise demos |
| 2 | Pilot implementations |
| 3 | Customer interviews |
| 4 | ROI measurement |
| 5 | Case study creation |
| 6 | Sales enablement |
| 7 | Partner onboarding |
| 8 | Investor conversations |

These activities are now **more likely to determine success** than another engineering milestone.

## Engineering role during this period

| Allowed | Not the priority |
|---------|------------------|
| Critical bugs | New modules / Phase 5 |
| Security updates | Speculative features |
| Performance fixes | Edge-family products |
| Customer-requested enhancements (evidence-gated) | Internal wishlist waves |
| TITAN ops/support/adoption execution | Cursor-led feature expansion |

**Tooling note:** Cursor (and similar) should not be the primary daily driver for the next 90–180 days. Use engineering capacity for freeze allow-list and TITAN execution only.
